# CL2.1 Collocation solver A3: results

2026-11-24, branch `laplace3d/cl2-1`. `CollocationSolver3d` in
`src/neural_operators/pde/poisson/collocation.rs` (behind `datagen`). The tests are in the
same file; every number below is printed by them
(`cargo test --release --features datagen --lib solver_3d -- --nocapture`).

## Accuracy

Relative max-norm error and relative 3D Clenshaw–Curtis L² error (`cc_rel_l2_error_nd`)
against the exact u on the cubic CGL grid n³. The tests assert ≤ 1e-12 from n = 33.

| u | n = 9 | n = 17 | n = 33 | n = 65 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sin πx sin πy sin πz | 1.4e-4 / 1.3e-4 | 3.0e-12 / 2.4e-12 | 4.0e-14 / 2.8e-14 | 1.1e-13 / 1.0e-13 |
| sin πx sin 2πy sin 3πz | 4.8e-1 / 5.4e-1 | 8.4e-5 / 6.8e-5 | 1.4e-14 / 6.3e-15 | 4.1e-14 / 2.7e-14 |
| (1−x²)eˣ(1−y²)(1−z²) | 3.7e-7 / 2.1e-7 | 4.3e-15 / 2.9e-15 | 4.3e-14 / 4.4e-14 | 3.3e-13 / 3.3e-13 |

Entries are max / CC L². The slow rise from n = 33 to 65 is the round-off of the
O(n⁴)-conditioned D² (κ grows about 16× per doubling), as in 2D.

Sine-series closed form (CL3.1's `grf3d`: f and u = Σ c / μ evaluated exactly at the nodes):

| K | n | seed | max | CC L² |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 33 | 0 | 3.8e-14 | 4.3e-14 |
| 8 | 33 | 7 | 4.2e-14 | 5.0e-14 |
| 16 | 65 | 1 | 1.8e-13 | 1.9e-13 |

Discrete residual of −(D_xx ×₁ + D_yy ×₂ + D_zz ×₃) U = F, applied without the
eigendecomposition and scaled by 3 ‖D‖_∞ ‖U‖ + ‖F‖: 6.0e-17 to 5.7e-18 for n = 9 … 65
(asserted ≤ 1e-14).

Non-cubic grid: (1−x²) sin πy sin 3πz reaches 1.5e-14 on [17, 25, 33], and 8.6e-5 with
the axes reversed, [33, 25, 17].

## Time per solve

Apple M2 Pro (10 cores), release build, ndarray (`matrixmultiply`), f64; `solve_full`
on a full n³ field, mean over 50 solves (n = 33) or 10 (n = 65); setup is
`CollocationSolver3d::new` (three 1D eigendecompositions). Three runs, taken while other
worktrees were compiling on the same machine:

| n | setup | solve | 12 (n − 2)⁴ flops (proposal §7) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 33 | 4.6–7.6 ms | 0.38–0.49 ms | 1.1e7 |
| 65 | 10–17 ms | 5.5–17.6 ms | 1.9e8 |

The quieter runs give about 30 GFLOP/s at both sizes, so the solve follows the n⁴ count.
At n = 65, the labels of a 1200-sample set take 7–21 s of solves on this machine.

Command: `cargo test --release --features datagen --lib time_per_solve_3d -- --ignored
--nocapture`. No timing is asserted in a test.
