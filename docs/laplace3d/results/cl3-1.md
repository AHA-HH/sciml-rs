# CL3.1 results: the 3D GRF's truncation error

2026-11-24, Mac (M2 Pro), `cargo test --features datagen --lib grf3d -- --nocapture`
(debug build; the numbers are exact sums, not timings). Code:
`src/neural_operators/pde/poisson/grf3d.rs`; plan
`docs/laplace3d/tasks/CL3.1.md`.

## S_∞ and ε_K (τ = 3, α = 3)

S_∞ = ⅛ Σ_{k,l,m ≥ 1} λ_klm = **1.232538e-4** (`grf3d::S_INF`): the cube sums to m = 80 and
160, with their (m + ½)⁻³ tail extrapolated (proposal §3.3). The tail beyond m = 160 is
5.0e-6 of S_∞; the same extrapolation from m = 40 and 80 differs by 7.9e-7, so the value
is good to about 2e-7 relative.

| K | n with K(n) = (n − 1)/2 | ε_K = 1 − S_K / S_∞ | proposal §3.3 | ≤ 5e-3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 17 | 2.742e-2 | 2.7e-2 | no |
| 16 | 33 | 4.265e-3 | 4.3e-3 | yes |
| 32 | 65 | 5.892e-4 | 5.9e-4 | yes |
| 64 | 129 | 7.703e-5 | 7.7e-5 | yes |

Every value agrees with the proposal's α = 3 row to its two digits. A first attempt
bounded the tail by the octant outside radius m + ½ (as the 2D test does); that bound
overestimates the 3D tail up to about 5×, an uncertainty of 9e-6 in ε, as large as ε_64
itself, so the extrapolation replaced it.

## Other checks (same run)

- Separable evaluation against the direct triple sum, 9 × 7 × 5 nodes, K = 4: max |diff|
  1.3e-15 at scale 2.85.
- −Δu − f by a 7-point stencil (h = 1e-3) at three interior points, K = 8: 2.0e-6 against
  max |f| 0.82 (the O(h²) truncation).
- Mean square of f_16 over 30 seeds: 0.958 ± 0.055 (expected 1).

## D3 re-proposed

τ = 3, α = 3, K(n) = (n − 1)/2: ε_K ≤ 5e-3 on f at both stages (4.3e-3 at n = 33, 5.9e-4
at n = 65), with margin 1.17 at n = 33. The alternative (α = 2, tolerance on u) is
unchanged from the proposal. The 3D draws use ChaCha8 stream 3 (`grf3d::STREAM`, `grf3d::RNG`),
seeds numbered as in 2D (`base_seed + i`).
