# CL3.3 results: error 2 of the n = 65 test splits, 2D and 3D

2026-11-26. Code: `src/neural_operators/data/loaders/poisson/uniform3d.rs` (the 3D loader
and the measuring test); plan `docs/laplace3d/tasks/CL3.3.md`. Roadmap: "error 2 of the
n = 65 test split at s = 32 and 64, against the 2D floor at the same s" (proposal §9; L1
gate C3).

## Method

`cargo test --release --lib
neural_operators::data::loaders::poisson::uniform3d::tests::error2_n65_against_2d --
--exact --ignored --nocapture`, on the Mac (Apple M2 Pro, 16 GB, release, host ndarray f64),
on the test splits `poisson_n65_K32_test` (2D, generated 2026-10-13) and
`poisson3d_n65_K32_test` (3D, CL3.2, 2026-11-25), all 200 samples, at the loader's commit on
`laplace3d/cl3-3`.

Each split is loaded at s by the uniform loader (`load_poisson_uniform`, or
`load_poisson3d_uniform`), whose targets are R u in physical units, and read raw on the CGL
grid by the Chebyshev reader (`load_poisson_cheb_field`, or `load_poisson3d_cheb_field`).
`poisson_errors_nd`, with the targets as the prediction, gives error 2 = ‖P R u − u‖ / ‖u‖
in the Clenshaw–Curtis norm (CONVENTIONS §12), per sample. The test asserts error 3 exactly
0 (the loader's targets are R u bit for bit) and error 4 equal to error 2, and at s = 64 the
mean and max equal to the sidecar's `error2` (the generator's, same transfers) to 1e-12
relative. All held.

## Error 2 (transfer floor), n = 65, K = 32, d = 2

| Field | s | mean | max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2D | 32 | 2.326e-4 | 8.932e-4 |
| 3D | 32 | 1.994e-4 | 4.559e-4 |
| 2D | 64 | 2.033e-5 | 7.809e-5 |
| 3D | 64 | 2.075e-5 | 4.663e-5 |

The 3D floor is at the 2D floor's level at both s: the means within 15% (3D 0.86× at s = 32,
1.02× at s = 64), the maxima lower in 3D (0.51×, 0.60×). Halving s raises the mean about
11× in 2D and 10× in 3D, near the d + 1 = 3 order of T_uc (8×). The two families differ
(2D α = 2, 3D α = 3 at τ = 3, proposal §3.3; 3D D3), so this is context, not a like-for-like
comparison: the smoother 3D forcing gives a smoother u, which offsets the third axis.

Load time of the 3D test split (read, check, T_cu on both fields, normalise; the test split
read twice, as train and test): 1.4 s at s = 32, 3.9 s at s = 64.
