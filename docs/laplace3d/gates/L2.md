## 2. Gate conditions and evidence

**A3 reaches the f64 floor.** `CollocationSolver3d` (`pde/poisson/collocation.rs`, behind
`datagen`) is proposal §5's A3: one interior D² eigendecomposition per axis, the two
three-way mode products by CL1.2's `apply_nd`, and the division by −(λ_i + μ_j + ν_k).
Relative max-norm and 3D Clenshaw–Curtis errors (`cc_rel_l2_error_nd`), asserted ≤ 1e-12
from n = 33:

| Solution | n = 33 (max / CC) | n = 65 (max / CC) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sin πx sin πy sin πz (manufactured) | 4.0e-14 / 2.8e-14 | 1.1e-13 / 1.0e-13 |
| sin πx sin 2πy sin 3πz (manufactured) | 1.4e-14 / 6.3e-15 | 4.1e-14 / 2.7e-14 |
| (1−x²)eˣ(1−y²)(1−z²) (manufactured) | 4.3e-14 / 4.4e-14 | 3.3e-13 / 3.3e-13 |
| `grf3d` sine series, K = 8 (seeds 0, 7) / K = 16 (seed 1) | 3.8e-14, 4.2e-14 / 4.3e-14, 5.0e-14 | 1.8e-13 / 1.9e-13 |

Spectral decay from n = 9 to 17 ≥ 100× on every manufactured solution; the discrete
residual, applied without the eigendecomposition, 6.0e-17 down to 5.7e-18 (n = 9 … 65); a
non-cubic grid [17, 25, 33] at 1.5e-14 (8.6e-5 with the axes reversed, where sin 3πz lies
on the 17-node axis: resolution, not error). The closed forms are CL3.1's `grf3d` series
(f and u = Σ c / μ evaluated exactly at the nodes), so the solver and the dataset's
forcing family are checked against each other before CL3.2 uses them.

Re-run here on `main` (`636298b`, Mac, release):
`cargo test --release --features datagen --lib solver_3d -- --nocapture`, 7 passed, every
number above reproduced.

**CI.** #136 green on all four jobs; `main` green after #136 (run 45594302551, `aabd87a`)
and after every later merge.

**Decisions.** L2 prepares none (no "Prepared by" entry names L2 or CL2.1).

## 5. Measured numbers against the proposal

Proposal §7 counts A3 at 12 (n − 2)⁴ flops per sample; CL2.1 measured (Apple M2 Pro,
release, ndarray `matrixmultiply`, f64, a machine busy with other builds):

| n | §7 flops | Solve | Setup (three 1D eigendecompositions) | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 33 | 1.1e7 | 0.38–0.49 ms | 4.6–7.6 ms | 22–29 GFLOP/s |
| 65 | 1.9e8 | 5.5–17.6 ms | 10–17 ms | 11–34 GFLOP/s |

- The solve follows the n⁴ count (about 30 GFLOP/s on the quieter runs at both sizes).
- Labels for a 1200-sample n = 65 set take 7–21 s of solves; CL3.2 measures the rest
  (the GRF evaluation, the label check, the writes).
- Accuracy rises from about 4e-14 at n = 33 to 1–3e-13 at n = 65, the round-off of D²'s
  O(n⁴) conditioning (κ about 16× per doubling), as in 2D. The 1e-12 bound holds with a
  3× margin at n = 65. The error grows 3–8× per doubling (2.75× for sin πx sin πy sin πz,
  about 4× for the `grf3d` series, 7.7× for (1−x²)eˣ(1−y²)(1−z²)), which puts n = 129 (D5,
  test-only if needed) between about 3e-13 and 2.5e-12: the worst case above that bound;
  unmeasured.

Corrections they imply: §7's solver row gives flops only; a measured time per solve beside
it would complete the table (C1).
